# Byline components

- `AppWindow` — floating product shell on the mint field.
- `IconRail` — five-destination desktop rail and mobile tab bar.
- `Greeting` — page greeting or title with a single optional action.
- `StatTile` — huge number, short label, note, and tactile seal.
- `PaperCard` — cream content surface.
- `InkWidget` — dark receipt, offer, or in-session surface.
- `HeatButton` — orange irreversible action only.
- `InkButton` — charcoal primary navigation or reversible action.
- `GhostButton` — quiet outlined action.
- `Row` — grouped portfolio-style row.
- `StatusDot` — live, asleep, or starved status.
- `ReceiptCard` — compact receipt row with `SIMULATED` local-state metadata.
- `Timeline` — eight-step job progress display.
- `ChainChecklist` — Add chain, Get RITUAL, Deposit dialog.
- `ToastStrip` — quiet product and local-mode notice.
- `EmptyState` — one sentence and one action.
